I am Simeon Taylor, a Liberian charcoal and pencil artist specializing in realistic portrait drawing. My work focuses on facial expression, character, emotion, and human stories.

My process begins with careful observation, proportion, structure, and tonal control. I gradually build values to create depth, texture, light, and realistic skin tones. The work is patient and detailed, with attention to the features that make each portrait personal.
